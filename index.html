02 · AI × your genres

Two ways to use AI

Make AI the topic of your Shorts, or use it as a tool to make them faster. YouTube allows AI-made videos. It stops paying for low-effort, mass-produced ones, so keep your voice and your opinion in every Short.

Prices come from published reviews and may change. YouTube's own AI clips carry an invisible SynthID watermark and an AI label.

05 · Stay monetized

Rules that protect your income

  1. Make it, don't re-upload it.Re-posted or lightly edited clips from other creators count as reused content and can't earn money.
  2. No templated mass production.Since July 16, 2026, YouTube won't pay for generic, repetitive or template-based videos, off-putting content, or AI personas advising on sensitive topics like health and finance. Channels built on these get removed from the Partner Program.
  3. Label realistic AI.Turn on "altered or synthetic content" when AI footage looks real. The label itself doesn't reduce reach.
  4. Watch the 1-minute line.A Short over 1 minute that gets a Content ID claim (game soundtracks, trailer music) gets blocked. Keep licensed audio clips under 60 seconds.
  5. Know the goal.1,000 subscribers plus 10 million Shorts views in 90 days (or 4,000 long-form watch hours) unlocks ad revenue.
